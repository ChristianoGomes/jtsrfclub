How often to service your board

Difficulty: Beginner Time: ~2 min Applies to: Gas boards Last updated: Sep 2026

Watch the walkthrough

Watch on YouTube ↗ · JETSURF USA — new owner series #20. Timestamps below follow the video's on-screen counter (0:26–1:11).

Three intervals cover it: an after-every-ride routine you do yourself, a short service every 6–10 hours, and a full service at the shop once a year.

After every ride — 0:26

  • Wash the whole board down with fresh water — especially after salt water. See Cleaning your board after a ride.
  • Lubricate any moving parts inside the engine compartment.
  • Check all the connections on the cables and pipes in the engine bay.
  • Flush the engine through with fresh water, then spray it with a spray lubricant.

Every 6–10 hours — 0:52

  • Change the spark plug. See Spark plug check & replacement.
  • Spray oil or lithium grease down the tube into the main bearing.

Every 50 hours or 12 months — 1:02

  • Send the board to your service center for a full yearly service.
Tip: track engine hours from day one — the 6–10 hour and 50 hour intervals only mean something if you know where you are. The JETSURF app logs hours; a note in your phone works too.
Caution: the yearly shop service isn't optional — it's the interval that catches bearing, seal, and drivetrain wear before it becomes a failure on the water. Book it at 50 hours or 12 months, whichever comes first.
