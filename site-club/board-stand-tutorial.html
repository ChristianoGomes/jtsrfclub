Setting up the board stand

Unfolding the stand so your board has somewhere to sit — a two-minute job once you know which legs are which.

Guide

Setting up the board stand

Difficulty: Beginner Time: ~2 min Applies to: All boards Last updated: Sep 2026

Watch the walkthrough

Watch on YouTube ↗ · JETSURF USA — new owner series #6. Timestamps below follow the video's on-screen counter (0:23–0:47).

The stand ships folded flat in its own bag. It opens out into an X — the trick is knowing the longer legs go on the bottom.

Steps

  1. 0:23 — Take the stand out of its bag.
  2. 0:33 — Twist the stand to one side. The longer legs are the bottom legs.
  3. 0:40 — Hold it upright, take two opposite corners, and open it out.
  4. 0:47 — Set it on the floor, longer legs down, ready for the board to sit on.
Tip: if the stand looks unstable or tips easily, you've likely opened it with the short legs on the bottom — fold it back and flip it.
Caution: set the stand on firm, level ground before you rest the board on it, and keep it clear of foot traffic — a board sliding off a stand takes gelcoat with it.
