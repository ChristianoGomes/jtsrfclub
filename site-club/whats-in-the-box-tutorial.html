What comes with your new board

Difficulty: Beginner Time: ~5 min Applies to: All boards Last updated: Sep 2026

Watch the walkthrough

Watch on YouTube ↗ · JETSURF USA — new owner series #1. Timestamps below follow the video's on-screen counter (0:31–2:15).

Everything ships inside the board bag. Work from the back of the board forward, and set each item aside as you unpack — a couple of these you'll need every time you ride, and one of them you can't start the board without.

What's in the box

  1. 0:31 · Foam protector — fits over the back of the board. Don't throw it away — you'll need it for transport and storage.
  2. 0:43 · Wheels — a set of wheels for the board bag, for wheeling it around.
  3. 0:47 · Stand — the stand for the board.
  4. 0:52 · Warranty & documents — a QR code to register the warranty, plus your certificate and other paperwork.
  5. 1:03 · User manual — packed at the front ("up") end of the board.
  6. 1:04 · Toolkit — in its own zip bag in the middle of the board (contents below).
  7. 2:06 · Fins — the main FCS fin plate, the main FCS fin, and two side fins.

Inside the toolkit

  • 4 mm hex screwdriver — fins and general bolts around the board
  • Spark plug spanner
  • Spare spark plug (in the compartments)
  • Flushing tool — for flushing the jet out
  • Throttle restrictor — if you want to limit the throttle
  • Spark plug testing tool
  • Spare nuts and bolts (two lots)
  • Spare lanyard
  • Charger with international plug adapters
  • Two magnet bracelets — this is what starts the board
Tip: keep the foam protector and both magnet bracelets somewhere you won't lose them. The foam protector goes back on for every transport; the magnet is the only way to start the board, so keep the spare separate from the one you ride with.
Caution: register the warranty from the QR code before you ride, and read the user manual first — a new board out of the bag still needs the fins fitted and a full pre-ride check.
