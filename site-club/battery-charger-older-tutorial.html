Battery charger (older JetSurfs)

Difficulty: Intermediate Time: ~5 min + charge Applies to: Older boards Last updated: Sep 2026

These are the older JETSURF USA tutorials for the multi-chemistry balance charger that shipped with 2018–2019 petrol boards. For the newer sealed charger and its modes, see Charging a petrol board.

What's in the charger box

  1. 0:24 — Charger manual.
  2. 0:28 — 12 V cigarette-lighter lead.
  3. 0:32 — European mains plug lead.
  4. 0:36 — US mains plug lead.
  5. 0:45 — Attached to the charger: a red-and-black lead ending in a two-pin connector — this plugs into the board.

The charger itself has three ports: the red-and-black board connection, a 12 V input, and a mains power-plug input.

Find the charging connector

  1. 0:17 — Open the charger's latches and set the lid aside. Connect the charger to power (US plug shown).
  2. 1:23 — On 2018 and 2019 models (e.g. the 2019 Adventure DFI), the charging connector is on the left rear of the board, marked with a red zip tie.
  3. 1:48 — It sits in a bracket with a clip on the outer edge — gently pull the clip back to release the connector.
  4. 2:08 — Push the charger's pink connector onto the board connector, lining up the clip with its slot. A click means it's secure.

Charge in LiPo mode

  1. 2:28 — At the charger, go to home so you can see all the chemistry icons — LiPo is the middle one. (The boards only ever use LiPo charging.) Select LiPo → LiPo charge.
  2. 2:52 — On the settings screen: 3S = the three cells in the battery. Voltage should read 4200 (4.20 V/cell).
  3. 3:04 — Set the charge current — recommended 3 A to 6 A depending on how much time you have. Set 3 A and press Start.
  4. 3:23 — Watch voltage (charge level in the iCU) and capacity (milliamps going into the battery). The charger balances all cells so each holds the right charge to power the board's units.
  5. 4:07 — Leave it until the charger shows "End" — it clicks and beeps. Charging is then complete.
Tip: charge after every session. If you can't wait for it to reach "End", give it at least 30–45 minutes — enough to keep the iCU healthy until the next full charge.
Caution: only ever use LiPo mode — the wrong chemistry setting can damage the battery. Confirm 3S and 4200 V before pressing Start, and never charge above 6 A. Use one power source at a time (mains or 12 V). Questions: call JETSURF USA.
