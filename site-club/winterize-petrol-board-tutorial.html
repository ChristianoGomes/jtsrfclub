Winterizing a petrol board

Dry it out, lube the engine bay, pull the fuel tank, prop the lid for airflow, and keep the battery topped up.

Winterizing a petrol board

Difficulty: Intermediate Time: ~30 min Applies to: Gas boards Last updated: Sep 2026

Watch the walkthrough

Watch on YouTube ↗ · JETSURF USA — new owner series #21. Timestamps below follow the video's on-screen counter (0:32–2:24).

Winterizing is what you do before the board sits unused for a stretch — a winter, or a long off-season. Start from a clean, dry board, get the fuel tank out, leave the bay lubricated and able to breathe, and keep the battery alive with a charge every 30 days.

Tools & supplies

  • Spray lubricant for the engine bay
  • The charger (for the monthly top-up)
  • A dry place to store the fuel tank, away from the board

Dry and lubricate

  1. 0:32 — Clean the board after your last ride (see Cleaning your board after a ride), then make sure it's completely dry with all the water out.
  2. 0:42 — Remove the engine lid. With a dry, clean bay, spray over it and lubricate every moving part — same as the after-every-ride routine.

Remove the fuel tank

  1. 1:00 — Disconnect the fuel line from the tank: push the stainless piece on the push-button connector in and it springs out.
  2. 1:19 — Undo the petrol line on the board, then the Velcro strap.
  3. 1:29 — Unclip and remove the plug on the side of the tank.
  4. 1:35 — Gently lift the tank out of the board.
  5. 1:41 — On the back of the tank, push the air breather in and pull its tube out.
  6. 1:48 — Store the tank somewhere dry, out of the way, away from the board.

Close it up for storage

  1. 1:52 — With the board dry, tank out, and lubricant sprayed over the bay, set the engine lid back on — either normally or offset — so air can flow through and condensation can't build up.
  2. 2:12 — Every ~30 days, charge the board to keep the battery and iCU maintained in their best condition. See Charging a petrol board.
Tip: the lid goes on loose or offset on purpose — a sealed engine bay traps moisture and corrodes over a long store. The point is airflow, not weather-sealing.
Caution: store the fuel tank separately from the board, dry and ventilated — never leave it fitted and full over winter. Don't skip the 30-day charge; a petrol board's battery and iCU degrade if left flat for months.
