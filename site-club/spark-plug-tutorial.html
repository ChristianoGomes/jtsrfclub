Spark plug check & replacement

Reading plug color, gapping, and swapping the spark plug on a gas JetSurf board without cross-threading the head.

Guide

Spark plug check & replacement

Difficulty: Intermediate Time: ~20 min Applies to: Gas boards Last updated: Sep 2026

Watch the walkthrough

Watch on YouTube ↗ · timestamps in the steps below follow the video's on-screen counter (0:00–0:55).

The spark plug is one of the fastest checks you can run on a gas board — do it before every few sessions, and any time the engine feels down on power or is hard to start.

Tools & supplies

  • Combination wrench (included with every JetSurf)
  • Replacement spark plug — correct heat range for your model
  • Clean rag

Steps

  1. 0:00 — Remove the spark plug cover and disconnect the connector.
  2. 0:02 — Using the combination wrench included with every JetSurf, loosen the spark plug.
  3. 0:12 — Unscrew the spark plug completely and remove it from the surfboard.
  4. 0:30 — Insert the (new) spark plug into the surfboard. Screw it in and tighten it using the wrench.
  5. 0:47 — Connect the spark plug boot and install the boot cover.
Tip: thread the plug in by hand first and turn it until it seats, then give it a final snug with the wrench. If it doesn't turn freely by hand, back it out and re-start the threads.
Caution: never force the wrench on the first few turns — that's how the head gets cross-threaded. Hand-start every time.
