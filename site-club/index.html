Know your board

JETSURF boards are carbon-hulled, direct-fuel-injection or electric powered, and hand-built for racing. Knowing how they're put together makes every service job easier.

  • Titanium DFI — gas, lightweight, race-proven
  • Electric — quiet, instant torque, zero fumes
  • Both rewarding to maintain once you know the routine
