Safety gear

The race kit

Required on a start line and worth wearing every session. Sizing and fit matter more than brand — try before you buy where you can.

Head

Helmet

Water-rated helmet with a secure chin strap and drain holes. Full-face for racing, open-face acceptable for training.

Stage MIPS Race helmet →
Head

Visor

Clear or tinted visor for the helmet — cuts spray and glare at speed. Match it to your helmet model.

4-FQ motocross visor →
Neck

Neck guard

Brace-style neck guard to spread the load of a high-speed water impact. Standard in every motosurf race class.

Board

Leash

Coiled leash worn on your arm that keeps the board with you after a fall — and the kill-switch magnet with it.

Body

Impact vest

Buoyancy plus rib and back protection, race-cut so it doesn't limit your reach on the board.
