Fuel injection removal & installation

Taking the throttle body and reed valve off, inspecting the reed petals against a light, and reassembling with fresh gaskets.

Guide

Fuel injection removal & installation

Difficulty: Intermediate Time: ~25 min Applies to: Gas boards Last updated: Sep 2026

Watch the walkthrough

Watch on YouTube ↗ · JETSURF Service Tutorials #9. Timestamps in the steps below follow the video's on-screen counter (0:14–0:36).

This is the job for inspecting or replacing the reed valve — the one-way flap valve behind the throttle body. Worn reed petals cost power and make the engine hard to start. The check itself takes seconds; the care is in the two gaskets on reassembly.

Tools & supplies

  • 3 mm Allen key
  • Two new gaskets (engine-to-reed and reed-to-throttle-body)
  • Thread-locker (Loctite)
  • Bright light source for the reed inspection
  • Replacement reed valve — only if the petals fail the light check

Removal

  1. 0:14 — Using a 3 mm Allen key, unscrew the throttle body.
  2. 0:14 — Remove the reed valve housing together with the throttle body.

Inspect the reed petals

  1. 0:22 — Hold the assembly up to a light source and look through the reed petals. If any light passes through, the petals are not sealing — replace the reed valve.

Installation

  1. 0:29 — Fit the first gasket onto the engine, then mount the reed valve.
  2. 0:33 — Place the second gasket between the reed valve and the throttle body.
  3. 0:36 — Position the throttle body and drive the bolts with Loctite.
Tip: a good reed petal sits dead flat against its seat — no daylight, no curl at the tip. Check both sides of every petal against the light, not just one.
Caution: always use both new gaskets and don't reuse the old ones — an air leak here leans the mixture and can cause engine damage. Loctite every bolt; never run them dry.
