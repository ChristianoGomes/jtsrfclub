Guide

Picking the right one

Read time: ~6 min Applies to: All boards Last updated: Sep 2026

Every JETSURF shares the same basic idea — a carbon hull, a small two-stroke or electric motor, and a jet pump instead of a prop. What actually separates the eight current boards is power source, hull shape, and whether you're standing free or holding a handlebar. Top speed is the number everyone asks about first, but it's rarely the number that should decide it.

Gas or electric — decide this first

The DFI (digital fuel injection) boards run on a small two-stroke engine and a 0.74-gallon tank — about 60 minutes on the water, refuel in a couple of minutes, and go again. Because the fuel tank and iCU can be removed, a gas board can also be flown as checked luggage with the right prep — see our flying with your board guide — which matters if you're travelling to ride.

The Electric 2 and Electric 2 Ski swap the engine and tank for a battery pack — quieter, no fuel smell, nothing to mix or spill. Expect 25–55 minutes per charge depending on how hard you ride, and a 2.5-hour full recharge. They're heavier than the gas boards because of the battery, and you're tied to wherever you can plug in. If you're riding all day from a dock or a lake house, that's a non-issue; if you're chasing spots or flying to ride, gas is the more flexible choice.

Match the hull to your skill level

The lineup is built as a ladder, not eight unrelated boards:

  • Adventure 2 DFI — the most popular board in the range for a reason. A stable, forgiving hull with dual bindings, so a second rider or a kid can go too. It's the board JTSRF CLUB puts new riders on first.
  • Cruiser DFI and Electric 2 — a step up in agility once Adventure feels easy, still dual-binding and still fine for a beginner who wants a faster-turning hull sooner.
  • Race DFI SL and Race DFI Ski — single binding, lighter, and noticeably quicker. Built for riders who are past the learning phase and want to carve or race, not just cruise.
  • Titanium DFI SL and Titanium DFI Ski — the fastest and lightest boards JETSURF makes, UIM certified and built for competition. Not a sensible first board.

The full spec comparison — top speed, weight, range, and every checkbox — is on the boards page.

Stand-up or handlebar (Ski)

Three boards in the range — Titanium DFI Ski, Race DFI Ski, and Electric 2 Ski — add a padded deck and a handlebar to the same hull and motor as their stand-up sibling. It's not a beginner-vs-advanced choice; it's a balance preference. Some riders, especially newer ones or kids stepping up from a Tube, find a handlebar easier to control while they build confidence. Others prefer having both hands free. If you're unsure, the stand-up version is easier to demo first.

Riding with family or as a first board

If you want a second person on the board with you, you need dual bindings — that's the Adventure 2 DFI, Cruiser DFI, or Electric 2. The Race and Titanium boards are single-binding, solo machines. Every board in the range is also compatible with the JETSURF Tube, a useful add-on for kids or anyone learning balance before they try to stand.

Racing

If competition is the goal, you want a UIM-certified board — currently the Race DFI SL, Titanium DFI SL, and Titanium DFI Ski. The Titanium DFI SL is the UIM's exclusive board for MotoSurf World Cup events. See our racing page for the series itself and how to get on a start line.

Three quick questions

  1. Have you ridden a motorized surfboard before? If not, start with the Adventure 2 DFI or Cruiser DFI — everything else assumes some time already on the water.
  2. Does quiet, fuel-free riding matter more than fast refuels? Electric if yes; DFI if you'd rather top up a tank in two minutes and keep going.
  3. Riding solo and pushing speed, or bringing someone with you? Solo and fast points at the Race or Titanium line; bringing a second rider points back to a dual-binding board.
