What you need to ride

The short list — fuel and oil, a buoyancy aid, a helmet, and a wetsuit if the water's cold.

Guide

What you need to ride

Difficulty: Beginner Time: ~2 min Applies to: Gas boards Last updated: Sep 2026

Watch the walkthrough

Watch on YouTube ↗ · JETSURF USA — new owner series #9. Timestamps below follow the video's on-screen counter (0:27–0:58).

Beyond the board itself, the ride kit is short. The only consumable is fuel; the rest is safety gear.

What you need

  1. 0:27 · Fuel and two-stroke oil — petrol mixed with two-stroke oil at 50:1.
  2. 0:35 · Buoyancy aid — always recommended.
  3. 0:35 · Helmet — a full water-sports helmet at a minimum. (JETSURF racers use a full-face helmet.)
  4. 0:43 · Wetsuit — depending on where you are and the water temperature.

Also recommended

  • 0:51 — The JETSURF tube — good for family and kids, and a useful learning aid.
Tip: pre-mix your fuel at 50:1 before you get to the water and label the can — mixing on the beach is where the ratio goes wrong. A dedicated ratio bottle makes it foolproof.
Caution: a buoyancy aid and a helmet are not optional — a jetsurf is a powered watercraft. Match the wetsuit to the water temperature, not the air temperature.
