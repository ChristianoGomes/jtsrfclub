Earlier models

Boards JETSURF has since replaced. Still ridden, still supported — parts and the maintenance guides all apply. Not in the comparison table above.

Digital fuel injection · earlier model

Race DFI

The board the Race DFI SL was built from. 2-stroke NG 100 DFI, a lightweight carbon-fibre hull developed with JETSURF's factory racers, Pro foot bindings, FCS click-in fins, and a silencer (59 dB). 18.5 kg, 55 km/h, 60 min on a tank. Racing use only.

Current version: Race DFI SL →

Digital fuel injection · earlier model

Adventure DFI Plus

The long-distance Adventure. An on-deck rack carries a 3.8 L external tank on top of the 2.8 L internal — up to 3 hours of range. Alternator-charged battery, NG 100 DFI, FCS fins, silencer (59 dB). 22 kg, 55 km/h, 120 kg max load. Owner's manual →

Current version: Adventure 2 DFI →

Digital fuel injection · earlier model

Adventure DFI

The Adventure before the Adventure 2 — an all-rounder for cruising, long-distance and family days. Long-distance hull, dual foot bindings, FCS fins, silencer (59 dB), intake screen. NG 100 DFI, 20 kg, 55 km/h, 120 kg max, 2.8 L / 60 min. Owner's manual →

Current version: Adventure 2 DFI →

Digital fuel injection · earlier model

Titanium DFI

The 2023 competition Titanium — the SL's predecessor. 100 cc DFI with a titanium exhaust, a rebuilt race hull, adjustable fin toe, and a carbon jet-pump-tunnel winglet for extra water flow. Pro bindings, FCS fins. 18.5 kg, 62 km/h, 40 min. UIM closed-course only — not for public waters, not sold in California.

Current version: Titanium DFI SL →

Electric · earlier model

JETSURF Electric

The Electric before the Electric 2. Full-carbon Race hull, dual bindings, a Booster button on the handle, and a GORE-TEX decompression chamber that keeps humidity out. Plug-in 3 kWh (59 Ah) battery, 25–55 min, 55 km/h, 31.9 kg. Gold winner, 2022 NY Product Design Awards. Owner's manual →

Current version: Electric 2 →
