Bilge pump removal & installation

One bolt, one connector — swapping the bilge pump out of its bracket.

Guide

Bilge pump removal & installation

Difficulty: Beginner Time: ~10 min Applies to: All boards Last updated: Sep 2026

Watch the walkthrough

Watch on YouTube ↗ · JETSURF Service Tutorials #12. Timestamps in the steps below follow the video's on-screen counter (0:00–0:51).

The bilge pump clears water from the hull. This is one of the easiest jobs on the board — a single bolt holds it in its bracket and there's one connector. Do it if the pump stops moving water or gets noisy.

Tools & supplies

  • The tool for the bracket bolt (Allen key or screwdriver, per your model)
  • Replacement bilge pump if you're swapping it

Removal

  1. 0:00 — Unscrew the bracket bolt.
  2. 0:20 — Carefully slide the pump out of the bracket and disconnect the connector.

Installation

  1. 0:33 — Connect the connector.
  2. 0:51 — Slide the pump into the bracket and secure it with the bolt.
Tip: before you refit, clear any hair or grit from the pump inlet screen and check the outlet hose is still seated — that's usually why a pump "stops working."
Caution: seat the pump fully in its bracket before tightening the bolt so it can't vibrate loose, and make sure the connector clicks home — a half-seated connector corrodes.
