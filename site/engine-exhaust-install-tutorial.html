Engine & exhaust installation

Setting the exhaust and engine back in the hull, dialing in coupler axial play, and refitting the throttle cable and covers.

Guide

Engine & exhaust installation

Difficulty: Advanced Time: ~60 min Applies to: Gas boards Last updated: Sep 2026

Watch the walkthrough

Watch on YouTube ↗ · JETSURF Service Tutorials #7. Timestamps in the steps below follow the video's on-screen counter (0:00–9:10).

This is the reassembly side of Engine & exhaust removal. The two parts that need care are the coupler axial play and the throttle cable range — get those wrong and the board won't run right. Finish with a throttle position sensor check.

Tools & supplies

  • Silicone spray
  • Lubricant / grease for the gasket
  • 10 mm angled ratchet wrench
  • 5 mm Allen key (coupler M5 screws)
  • 4 mm Allen key (coupler lock screw, throttle cap)
  • 3 mm Allen key (sprocket cover)
  • Bent-nose pliers (exhaust springs)
  • Magnet or diagnostic tool (TPS calibration check)

Exhaust & engine

  1. 0:00 — Lubricate the sealing O-rings with silicone spray.
  2. 0:37 — Slide the exhaust into the hull. Make sure the exhaust spring bracket is correctly aligned with the engine.
  3. 1:00 — Carefully lower the engine into the hull.
  4. 1:42 — Apply lubricant to the gasket between the engine and exhaust.
  5. 2:18 — Support the engine and align it with the turbine shaft so the coupler can be installed freely.
  6. 2:56 — Secure the engine to the hull with the four nuts, tightening them with a 10 mm angled ratchet wrench.

Coupler & axial play

  1. 3:40 — Install the coupler and set the axial play: push the freewheel as far back as possible by hand and hold it there.
  2. 3:47 — With the freewheel held back, tighten the coupler using the two M5 hex screws and a 5 mm Allen key, working them alternately.
  3. 4:30 — Tighten the coupler locking screw with a 4 mm Allen key.

Exhaust springs, electrical & throttle

  1. 4:43 — Using bent-nose pliers, attach the two exhaust springs and connect the water supply hose.
  2. 5:40 — Reconnect the electrical connectors, and fasten the ground wire to the starter with Allen key no. 3.
  3. 7:47 — Route the throttle cable under the freewheel, running it between the engine plate and the engine. Seat the barrel end of the cable in the throttle lever's crescent-shaped slot.
  4. 8:00 — Fit the cable lock cap and tighten it with a 4 mm Allen key.
  5. 8:05 — Test throttle movement by hand for full range. If it's off, loosen the cap and re-adjust.

Covers & final check

  1. 9:00 — Install the sprocket cover with a 3 mm Allen key.
  2. 9:10 — Use a magnet or diagnostic tool to verify the throttle position sensor calibration is correct.
Tip: the axial-play setup (steps 7–9) is the one to slow down on — the freewheel must be pushed fully back and held while the M5 screws go in alternately, or the coupler ends up preloaded.
Caution: don't ride until the throttle passes both checks — full mechanical range by hand, and a correct TPS calibration reading. Confirm the water supply hose is connected before running the engine.
