How it works

From the dock to standing up

Book & brief

Reserve a slot, meet your coach at the marina, and run through the board, throttle, and safety leash on land.

Water start

You start in the water, ride prone, move to your knees, then stand — the coach is right beside you the whole time.

Ride & repeat

Lock in the fundamentals, then come back for coaching sessions or join open rides once you're cleared.

Good to know

Questions, answered

Do I need any experience?

None. The Intro Lesson is built for people who've never been on a board. If you can swim and hold onto a handle, you can start.

What should I bring?

Swimwear, a towel, sunscreen, and a sense of humor. We provide the board, safety vest, helmet, and leash.

How fit do I need to be?

Moderately. There's some pulling yourself onto the board and balancing. If you're comfortable in open water for 20–30 minutes, you're fine.

Where do we meet?

At our launch in Miami — you'll get the exact marina, parking notes, and a map link in your booking confirmation.

What happens if the weather is bad?

If conditions aren't safe we reschedule you at no charge or issue a full refund. Light rain usually still runs.

Can I book for a group?

Yes. Message us with your group size and date and we'll set up back-to-back slots or a private block.

Is there an age or weight limit?

Riders should be roughly 13+ (minors with a guardian) and within the board's rated weight range. Contact us if you're unsure.
